    Easy Recipes

    Garlic Butter Pasta

    Ingredients

    • 1 serving pasta
    • 2 tbsp butter
    • 1–2 cloves garlic (minced) or ½ tsp garlic powder
    • Salt & pepper
    • Optional: Parmesan, red pepper flakes

    Instructions

    1. Boil pasta in salted water per package.
    2. Melt butter in a pan on low; add garlic 30–60s (don’t brown).
    3. Drain pasta, toss in butter, season, add optional toppings.
    Egg & Cheese Breakfast Sandwich

    Ingredients

    • 1 egg
    • 1 slice cheese
    • 1 English muffin or toast
    • 1 tsp butter
    • Salt & pepper

    Instructions

    1. Toast the bread.
    2. Cook egg in butter to preference; top with cheese to melt.
    3. Assemble on toast; season. Optional: hot sauce or ketchup.
    Veggie Quesadilla

    Ingredients

    • 1 large flour tortilla
    • ½–1 cup shredded cheese
    • ½ cup sliced peppers/onions
    • 1 tsp olive oil, salsa (for serving)

    Instructions

    1. Sauté peppers/onions in oil 2–3 min until soft.
    2. Place tortilla in pan, add cheese + veggies; fold.
    3. Cook 1–2 min per side until golden and melty. Slice and serve with salsa.

    Medium Recipes

    Chicken Veggie Stir-Fry

    Ingredients

    • 2 cups mixed veggies (bell pepper, broccoli, etc.)
    • 1 lb chicken breast, sliced thin
    • 2 cloves garlic, 1 tsp grated ginger
    • 3 tbsp soy sauce, 1 tsp sesame oil, 1 tsp cornstarch
    • Cooked rice for serving

    Instructions

    1. Toss chicken with 1 tbsp soy + cornstarch.
    2. Stir-fry chicken 3–4 min; remove.
    3. Stir-fry veggies 3–4 min; add garlic & ginger 30s.
    4. Return chicken, add remaining soy + sesame oil; toss to coat.
    5. Serve over rice.
    Lemon Herb Baked Salmon

    Ingredients

    • 2 salmon fillets
    • 1 lemon (zest & slices)
    • 1 tbsp olive oil, 1 tbsp butter (optional)
    • 1 clove garlic, chopped parsley
    • Salt & pepper

    Instructions

    1. Heat oven to 400°F/205°C. Line tray.
    2. Season salmon; top with oil, lemon zest/slices, parsley, garlic.
    3. Add butter on top (optional). Bake 10–12 min until flaky.
    Turkey Chili

    Ingredients

    • 1 lb ground turkey
    • 1 onion, 2 cloves garlic, 1 bell pepper (diced)
    • 1 can crushed tomatoes (14–15 oz), 1 can kidney beans (drained)
    • 1–2 tbsp chili powder, 1 tsp cumin, salt & pepper
    • 1 tbsp olive oil, 1 cup chicken stock (or water)

    Instructions

    1. Sauté onion/pepper in oil 3–4 min; add garlic 30s.
    2. Brown turkey, breaking up, 4–5 min; season.
    3. Add tomatoes, beans, stock, spices; simmer 20–25 min. Adjust seasoning.

    Hard Recipes

    Margherita Pizza (from scratch)

    Ingredients

    • Dough: 2½ cups flour, 1 tsp yeast, 1 tsp salt, 1 cup warm water, 1 tbsp olive oil
    • Toppings: ¾ cup crushed tomatoes, 8 oz fresh mozzarella, basil, olive oil, salt

    Instructions

    1. Mix dough, knead 5–7 min; rise 60–90 min until doubled.
    2. Heat oven to 500°F/260°C (stone if possible).
    3. Shape dough 12", top with tomatoes, mozzarella, basil, oil, salt.
    4. Bake 8–12 min until blistered and golden.
    Beef Bourguignon

    Ingredients

    • 2 lb beef chuck, cubes; 4 oz bacon
    • 1 tbsp flour, 1 tbsp tomato paste
    • 1 cup red wine, 2 cups beef stock
    • Onion, carrot, garlic, mushrooms
    • Butter/oil, thyme, bay leaf, salt & pepper

    Instructions

    1. Brown bacon; remove. Brown beef in batches.
    2. Sauté onion/carrot; add garlic, tomato paste, flour; cook 1 min.
    3. Deglaze with wine; add stock, beef, bacon, herbs. Simmer covered 2–3 hrs until tender.
    4. Sauté mushrooms in butter; stir in for last 10 min. Adjust seasoning.
    Chicken Parmesan

    Ingredients

    • 2 chicken cutlets (pounded ½″)
    • ¼ cup flour, 1 beaten egg, ½ cup breadcrumbs
    • ½ cup tomato sauce
    • ¼ cup grated Parmesan, ½ cup mozzarella
    • Olive oil, salt & pepper, basil (optional)

    Instructions

    1. Season chicken; dredge in flour, egg, then breadcrumbs.
    2. Pan-fry in oil 3–4 min/side until golden and ~165°F.
    3. Top with sauce, Parmesan, mozzarella; broil 1–3 min to melt. Garnish with basil.
